# Theory of Change

> Fill this in. This is the single most important document in the repo —
> it forces you to articulate WHY you're doing this and WHAT you'll contribute.

## 1. Which AI risks concern me most?
(e.g., misuse, misalignment, systemic concentration of power — pick 1–2)

## 2. Which technical bottleneck do I want to work on?
(e.g., mechanistic interpretability, scalable oversight, dangerous-capability evals)

## 3. What is my comparative advantage?
(What can I do that other safety researchers can't? Coding? Writing? Domain knowledge?)

## 4. What does success look like in 3 years?
(Be specific: role, contribution, paper, team)

## 5. What will make me quit?
(Honest answer — knowing your limits prevents burnout)
